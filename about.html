Food and event essentials for every function.

Ashu Catering Service provides food catering and event equipment, including marquees and chairs, for weddings, funeral functions and other gatherings. We aim to help customers organise the essentials they need for their function.

We serve functions across Matara, Galle and Colombo. Tell us the occasion, date, location, guest count and whether you need food, marquees, chairs or other event equipment.
